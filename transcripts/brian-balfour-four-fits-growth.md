## Why Product-Market Fit is Not Enough

Lenny Rachitsky: Welcome back. Today my guest is Brian Balfour, founder and CEO of Reforge, former VP of Growth at HubSpot. Brian, you famously coined the "Four Fits Framework". Why is traditional product-market fit insufficient to build a \$100M+ company?

Brian Balfour: Founders obsess over Product-Market Fit (PMF) in isolation, as if building a great product for a target audience is the whole battle. But product-market fit only answers whether someone wants what you built. It doesn't answer how you acquire them profitably, how you charge them, or whether the economics work.

Companies that scale to \$100M+ don't just have Product-Market Fit. They have four interrelated fits that constantly influence each other:
1. Market-Product Fit
2. Product-Channel Fit
3. Channel-Model Fit
4. Model-Market Fit

If any one of these four gears is jammed, your growth will stall out regardless of how much users love your features.

## Deep Dive into the Four Fits

### 1. Market-Product Fit
You start with the market—the problem space, the target audience, and their urgent pain points. You do not build a product and then hunt for a market. The market dictates the product attributes. You know you have Market-Product Fit when retention curves flatten parallel to the x-axis, showing true cohort stabilization.

### 2. Product-Channel Fit
Products are built to fit channels, channels do not mold to fit products. For example, Google and SEO are a channel with strict algorithmic rules; you must build a product with indexing architecture and public content loops to win there. Similarly, virality requires low friction, instant onboarding, and natural two-sided value. You cannot take an enterprise database that requires 6 weeks of onboarding and expect it to grow virally on TikTok or Twitter.

### 3. Channel-Model Fit
Your channel is dictated by your monetization model, specifically your Average Annual Contract Value (ACV) and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):
- If your ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) is \$10 to \$100 per year, your channels MUST be low-to-zero CAC: viral loops, word of mouth, or programmatic UGC SEO. You cannot afford sales reps or high CPC paid ads.
- If your ACV is \$100k+ per year, you cannot rely on organic virality alone; you need enterprise account-based marketing (ABM), field sales, and executive outbound.

### 4. Model-Market Fit
Model-Market Fit is the mathematical reality of your target market. If your target market has only 1,000 potential enterprise customers worldwide, you cannot charge \$1,000 a year and reach a \$100M business. You need an ACV of \$100,000+ with expansion revenue. Conversely, if you charge \$10/month, you need millions of potential users in a massive consumer or prosumer market.
